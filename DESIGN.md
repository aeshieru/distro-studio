# Design System: BEAT

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Komiks Weekly, night edition"**

A posted reel is read as a komiks strip. Every beat is a panel cut from the real video, framed square in a hairline violet-grey keyline, captioned in a hand-lettered face, and the panel where viewers left gets a white speech balloon. Underneath runs the retention curve on the same time axis, filled with violet halftone dots so the drop reads as a cliff in print. The product is BEAT: Brief, Explore, Arrange, Track, and the rail carries those four letters.

The night edition sets the temperature, taken from the user's reference (paralleldistribution.com/careers): a near-black violet ground, a deep navy shell for the rail and top bar, violet and lilac as the only accent, with a soft violet glow marking whatever is selected or current. Controls are pills; the one primary action is a white pill. The page declares `color-scheme: dark`. The komiks grammar lives in the content (panels, halftone, balloons, hand captions), while the working chrome around it stays quiet and rounded.

Density is a working-desk density: 14px body, 4 to 28px spacing, tabular numerals wherever numbers sit. Motion is small and eased on one curve (balloons and result panels pop in, panels lift 2px on hover, the guide cue breathes) and drops to near zero under reduced motion.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Near-black violet ground, deep navy shell, violet and lilac accent with a soft violet glow on selection.
- Pill controls and a white primary pill; rounded 12 to 16px working containers.
- Square-framed story containers (beat panels, takes, board cards, player) with real frame grabs under a halftone overlay.
- White speech balloons in Patrick Hand SC; hand-lettered captions in violet-dark caption boxes.
- Coral only for the drop, blocked checks and "after the drop".
- Self-hosted fonts via @fontsource; nothing loads off-site.

## Colors

A night print palette: violet-black ground, navy chrome, one cool violet accent that glows, and one warm coral held back for bad news.

### Primary
- **Electric Violet** (`violet`): the selection highlight, the halftone dots under the retention curve, the radio and range accent, the current stepper number, the player badge, the rendered status rim and the drop-target lane ring. It glows rather than fills: its translucent steps (`violet-glow-faint` to `violet-glow-strong`) are the soft halos on the selected option, the current nav letter, the current step, the dragged-over lane and the dropzone; `violet-edge` and `violet-rim` are the borders and top rims of tinted and raised containers.
- **Lilac** (`lilac`): links, focus rings, the primary-button hover, the "now" ring on the playing panel and selected card (`lilac-glow`, `lilac-glow-strong` for its halo), the new-hook landing line on the chart, pass text and completed steps.
- **Violet Tint** (`violet-tint`): the filled state of anything good or chosen: the selected option, the good chip, rendered tags, pass icons, the current step, the live AI pill, the howto line.

### Tertiary
- **Drop Coral** (`drop`): the drop line and flag on the chart, the outline on the drop panel, the fill on failed checks, the rejected tag rim, the bin lane on drag-over, the bad toast rim. **Drop Text** (`drop-text`) carries the same meaning as text on dark. **Drop Tint** (`drop-tint`) is the band where the hook was still talking after viewers left, and the bad chip fill; `drop-edge` and `drop-glow` are its border and halo.

### Neutral
- **Violet Night** (`ground`): page ground, sticky action bars, the chart label halo. `ground-veil` covers burned-in captions on the player; `halftone-dot` is the dot over every frame grab.
- **Navy Shell** (`shell`), **Shell Raised** (`shell-raised`) for hover on the rail, **Shell Sheen** (`shell-sheen`) the midpoint of the top bar's horizontal gradient, **Shell Deep** (`shell-deep`) the top stop of the stepper and dropzone gradient that fades into `surface`.
- **Surfaces** (`surface`, `surface-2`, `surface-3`): containers and inputs; panel frames and headers; hover fills, toasts, scrollbar thumbs and inline code.
- **Lines** (`line`, `line-2`): container borders and dividers; frames, control borders and dashed rules.
- **Text** (`text`, `text-2`, `text-3`) for reading, meta and placeholders; **White** (`white`) for headings, numbers and the primary pill.
- **Caption** (`caption`): caption boxes, hook quotes and the current-beat box.
- **Chart Ink** (`chart-ink`): the curve, dots, gridlines and playhead in the retention chart.
- **Balloon Ink** (`balloon-ink`) and **Button Ink** (`button-ink`): the only dark ink on white, for balloons and for primary-pill text. **Screen Black** (`screen-black`): the 9:16 video letterbox.
- **Float Shadow** (`float-shadow`): the toast's drop shadow.

### Named Rules
**The One Coral Rule.** Coral means exactly three things: the drop, a blocked or failed check, and "after the drop". Nothing else is ever coral: not emphasis, not decoration.

**The Glow Means Chosen Rule.** A violet glow marks the one thing that is current or selected (option, step, nav letter, now panel, drop target). If two things glow in the same group, one is wrong.

**The Data Has A Source Rule.** Reported points are solid dots; estimates between them are dashed; a full imported export is a solid line. The legend names which is which and where the numbers came from.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo (with Helvetica Neue, Arial)
**Body Font:** Public Sans (with Helvetica Neue, Arial), tabular numerals on by default
**Hand Font:** Patrick Hand SC (with Comic Neue, Chalkboard SE)
**Mono:** system ui-monospace, for paths and inline code only

**Character:** A sturdy grotesk for heads, numbers and controls, a plain civic sans for reading, and a komiks hand that speaks only inside the story.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, 30px, 1.1, -0.02em): the page title.
- **Headline / Timecode** (800 21px; 700 22px): title-row stats and the player timecode.
- **Title** (700, 16px): section heads ("Hook rewrite", "What Claude found"); option text sits just under at 700 15px.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.5, tabular): all reading text; verdicts capped at 80ch, guide and howto lines at 90 to 92ch. Body small (13px) for idea steps, checklists and tile text.
- **Control** (600, 13.5px Archivo): buttons, toasts, the crumb.
- **Meta / Label** (12.5px Public Sans; 600 12px Archivo): meta lines, field labels, small section labels. Sentence case. Micro, tag and badge (11.5, 11, 10.5px) for rail labels, status tags and the player badge.
- **Hand** (Patrick Hand SC 400 at 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 22px): caption boxes, the current-beat box, word-by-word preview captions, balloons, hook quotes on cards and in the card detail.

### Named Rules
**The Hand Speaks Only In The Strip Rule.** Patrick Hand SC is for captions, balloons and hook quotes. Never for buttons, labels, numbers or chrome.

**The Two Clocks Rule.** Positions on the reel (beat ranges, the drop, playhead, axis ticks) are m:ss, floored like a timecode. Durations and measured times are seconds with one decimal and a spaced unit ("5.4 s").

## Layout

An 80px navy rail on the left (B, E, A, T) and a sticky 56px navy top bar; content in a padded page (22px 28px 44px). Track is a two-column grid: the strip and chart take the flexible column, a 380px side column holds the 9:16 player and the rewrite behind a hairline rule, and ten equal framed take tiles run under the strip. Beat panel widths are proportional to beat seconds and share the chart's time axis exactly. When there is a next step, the stepper guide sits above everything and the before/after result panel above the strip. Explore is a 360px form column beside an auto-fill card grid (220px minimum); Arrange is four lanes plus a 200px bin; Brief is a two-column form capped at 1100px under the upload dropzone and Claude's draft.

At 1180px the side column drops under the main column, takes go 5 across, the board 2 lanes, before/after 2 across. At 720px the rail becomes a horizontal sticky bar, the strip scrolls sideways (900px minimum), takes go 2 across and every form goes single column, with safe-area insets on the edges.

## Elevation & Depth

Tonal layering plus glow. Surfaces step up from ground to `surface`, `surface-2` and `surface-3`, separated by hairlines; raised working containers get a violet top rim. Shadows are not used for lift: depth on selection is a soft violet (or lilac) glow, and only the toast casts a real shadow because it floats over content.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Now ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #a684ff, 0 0 16px rgba(166, 132, 255, 0.35)`; 0.3 on cards): the panel under the playhead, the selected board card.
- **Selection glow** (`box-shadow: 0 0 22px rgba(142, 81, 255, 0.18)`): the chosen rewrite option; the current nav letter uses `0 0 18px rgba(142, 81, 255, 0.45)`, the current step `0 0 18px rgba(142, 81, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Drop target** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1.5px #8e51ff, 0 0 24px rgba(142, 81, 255, 0.2)`; inset coral on the bin; `0 0 28px rgba(142, 81, 255, 0.25)` on the dropzone).
- **Toast** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), 0 0 24px rgba(142, 81, 255, 0.25)`; coral glow when bad).

### Named Rules
**The Glow Not Lift Rule.** Nothing rises on a drop shadow. A surface separates by tone and hairline; a chosen thing separates by violet glow.

## Shapes

Two shape families. Story containers are square: beat panels, take tiles, board cards, idea cards, the player screen and the before/after videos have 0 radius and a 1.5px solid `line-2` frame. Working chrome is rounded: pills (999px) for buttons, selects in the bar, chips, status tags, stepper steps, the AI pill and the toast; 16px for panels, the stepper, result, draft and dropzone; 14px for lanes, options and empty states; 12px for nav items and howto lines; 10px for inputs, nav letters and the lane drop hint; 8px for the current-beat box; 6px for inline code and scrollbar thumbs; 4px for the chart's drop flag. Balloons are full ellipses with a skewed ink tail. Empty states, the bin lane and the dropzone use dashed frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** pill (999px), 1px `line-2` border, Archivo 600 13.5px, 8px 16px.
- **Default:** transparent with white text; hover fills `surface-3` and lifts the border to `text-3`.
- **Primary:** white pill, button-ink text; hover turns lilac. One primary per action group.
- **Ghost:** no border, 6px 10px; hover fills `surface-3`. **Link button:** lilac underlined text inside guide copy.
- **Focus:** 2px lilac outline, 2px offset. Disabled at 45% opacity.

### Chips
- **Style:** pill, 1px `line-2` border, 12px Public Sans 500 on `surface`.
- **State:** good chips are violet-tint with a violet edge and white text; bad chips (blocked, after the drop) are coral tint with drop text.

### Cards / Containers
- **Working containers** (panel, detail, stepper, result, draft): 16px radius, 1px `line` border with a violet top rim, `surface` fill (stepper and dropzone fade from `shell-deep`), 16 to 18px padding.
- **Story containers** (beat panel, take tile, board card, idea card): square, 1.5px frame, `surface` or `surface-2` faces, a hand caption strip, 7 to 10px padding.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** `surface` fill, 1px `line-2` border, 10px radius, 9px 12px, Public Sans 500 14px; textareas grow with content. Labels sit above in Archivo 600 12.5px.
- **Focus:** lilac focus ring. Radios and ranges use the violet accent.

### Navigation
- **Rail:** navy, the BEAT wordmark at top, then four items each a 36px rounded letter box over a label. Hover fills shell raised; the current letter fills violet tint with a violet border and glow.
- **Top bar:** navy gradient through shell sheen, crumb in Archivo, a pill select and the AI status pill (lilac dot glows when live) at the right.

### Beat Panel (signature)
A square-framed panel with a 124px real frame grab under a 4px halftone dot overlay, then a caption box in Patrick Hand SC with the beat id and m:ss range in small lilac Archivo above. It lifts 2px on hover, takes the lilac now ring under the playhead and a coral outline when it holds the drop.

### Speech Balloon (signature)
A white ellipse with a 1.5px balloon-ink frame and skewed tail, Patrick Hand SC, popping in on the shared ease. Above the strip it marks where viewers left; over the player it carries the previewed hook word by word, the current word in violet.

### Retention Chart (signature)
The curve sits on the strip's axis over a 6px rotated violet halftone fill, chart-ink curve 2.2px, faint gridlines, the old hook end as a dashed line, the new hook's landing as a lilac line, the drop as a coral line with a coral flag, and the scrubbing playhead in chart ink.

### Stepper Guide
A rounded strip above Track naming the steps as numbered pills: done steps lilac with a check, the current step violet-tint with a glowing violet number, later steps faint. One plain sentence beside it says what to do now; the target control gets a breathing dashed lilac cue.

### Before / After Result
A rounded panel that pops in with two framed 9:16 videos side by side (200px each), captioned, then a facts list (dt in 12px Archivo `text-3`, dd in 14px body, key numbers lilac) and a link-styled download button.

### Upload Dropzone and Draft Review
On Brief, a dashed 1.5px rounded dropzone fading from shell deep; dragging over turns its border violet with a soft glow. Claude's draft appears below as a rounded panel with a two-column facts list and its own action row, labelled to be checked before use.

### Howto Line
A single violet-tint line with a violet edge, 12px radius, 14px body text and a bold Archivo lead ("What to do:"), placed at the top of each screen's form.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** frame every story container (panel, take, card, player) square in a 1.5px `line-2` keyline.
- **Do** use real frame grabs inside beat panels, under the halftone overlay.
- **Do** round working chrome: pills for controls, 10 to 16px for fields and containers.
- **Do** make the one primary action a white pill.
- **Do** mark the current or selected thing with violet tint and a soft violet glow.
- **Do** keep coral for the drop, blocked or failed checks and "after the drop" only.
- **Do** draw reported points as dots, estimates as dashed lines, and name the source of every number.
- **Do** write positions as m:ss and durations as one-decimal seconds ("5.4 s").
- **Do** self-host every font through @fontsource.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use coral for emphasis or decoration.
- **Don't** set Patrick Hand SC on controls, labels, numbers or chrome.
- **Don't** lift surfaces with drop shadows; use tone, hairlines and glow.
- **Don't** round story containers; rounding belongs to chrome.
- **Don't** add a second accent hue beside violet and lilac.
- **Don't** draw a retention curve that was not reported or imported.
